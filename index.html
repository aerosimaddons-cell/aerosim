Upload rules
  • At least 1 screenshot of the mod (up to 8). JPG, PNG or WebP, 5 MB each. The first one is the cover.
  • Upload a PC file (.zip, .rar, .7z), an Android file (.tme), or both. 1 GB each at most.
  • Description of at least 30 characters.
  • Only your own work or mods you have permission to share. Paid mods need your Patreon link, and buyers pay you there. No ripped or malicious files.
  • One post per mod name for each creator.
